dependency metadata, local imports, source integrity, and documentation.

Successful compilation and the exact axiom dependencies of public theorems
have not been certified by this release audit.

Static source scan

The scan found no sorry, admit, project axiom declarations, or unsafe
declarations in the packaged Lean files. This is a source-text check, not a
compiled proof or axiom audit. Dependencies are external packages pinned by

native_decide uses compiled evaluation and therefore has a different trust
profile from proof terms checked solely by the kernel. These finite checks
are not themselves all-parameter evaluations of the graph formulas. Their
presence in an imported file does not establish that every public theorem
depends on them. After building, use #print axioms on each theorem of
interest to inspect its actual transitive axiom dependencies. This release
makes no claim that every endpoint has only a particular standard axiom set.

Mathematical scope

The declarations and explicit hypotheses in the Lean sources determine the
precise theorem scope. The Core4 corrected raw endpoints concern the binary
field F2 finite-assignment model; the paper-facing corrected statements for
Triangle plus leaf, Diamond, and K4 require n >= 2 and positive block sizes.
Internal theorems may have different or stronger hypotheses and conclusions.
See each project's README for its entry points.

The raw Delta = 2^X results do not, by themselves, formalize the physical
contraction-to-assignment correspondence or connected-support subtraction
needed to obtain the paper's Gamma_H functions. This package does not claim
a Lean-certified derivation of those paper/model bridges from the raw
endpoints. Historical exponent definitions are retained for provenance;
use the corrected public endpoints identified in the Core4loop README.

Integrity

SHA256SUMS records the packaged files' contents. A passing hash check detects
changes relative to that manifest; it does not prove mathematical validity,
successful compilation, or the identity of the publisher.
