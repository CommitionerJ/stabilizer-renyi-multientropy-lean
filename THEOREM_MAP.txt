Public theorem map and scope

Paths below are relative to the archive root. Names and hypotheses were read
from the included Lean declarations. This map does not claim a new compilation
or axiom audit; see VERIFICATION.txt. n and block sizes are natural numbers.
[NeZero n] means n is nonzero. F2 is the two-element field ZMod 2.

Interpretation limits
  The four Core4 endpoints evaluate the binary, un-packaged finite-assignment
  signed sums DeltaNF (logical level 7, raw Delta=2^X). They quantify over all
  positive blocks and the indicated n range; they are not finite-enumeration
  substitutes. Use the corrected exponents for the three named corrected
  endpoints. Historical formulas are retained for provenance and may differ.
  Core4 proper-support subtraction, full-support Gamma extraction, and the
  manuscript contraction-to-assignment bridge are not certified by these raw
  endpoint statements or by this packaging audit.
